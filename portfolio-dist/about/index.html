About this site

All 20 projects catalogued here have a live demo you can open. Every image on the site is a screenshot of the project it belongs to, taken at 1440px and at 390px.

Where a demo runs on fixture data, or a page was never finished, the project page says so in plain words instead of hiding it. The site itself is an Angular application, prerendered to static HTML, with self-hosted fonts and no third-party requests at runtime.
